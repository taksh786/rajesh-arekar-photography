About the studio

Founder & Lead Photographer

Rajesh Arekar

Rajesh has been behind the camera for over 30 years. He started in the days of film rolls and negatives, watched photography evolve, and grew with it.

Along the way he has worked with well-known brands, personalities and names across industries. His journey has been shaped by a love for photography, the people he has met and the stories he has captured.

30+ years · Weddings, corporate & events

Photographer & Brand Strategist

Yash Arekar

Yash has been passionate about photography for over 12 years. With a background in marketing and brand strategy, he brings creativity together with an understanding of how brands communicate through visuals.

After more than five years in the family business, he specialises in food, ambience and wedding photography, and looks past the good picture to the story, the mood and the small details that make a photograph stand out.

12+ years · Food, ambience & weddings
