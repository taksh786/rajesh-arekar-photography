About

Rajesh Arekar

Founder · 30+ years

Rajesh has been behind the camera for over 30 years. He started in the days of film rolls and negatives, watched photography change, and changed with it.

Over the years he has worked with well-known brands, personalities and names across industries. For him, photography has always been more than taking pictures. It is about seeing things differently.

Yash Arekar

Photographer · 12+ years

Yash brings a background in marketing and brand strategy, and an understanding of how brands communicate through pictures.

He has worked alongside his father in the family business for over five years and specialises in food, interiors and weddings, paying attention to the story, the mood and the small details.
